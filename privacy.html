School Planner: Family — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

School Planner: Family is designed to help parents and families organise school schedules, activities, wraparound care and calendar events.

Information Stored by the App

School Planner may store information entered by you, such as children's names, school schedules, activities, clubs and planning information.

This information is used to provide the app's planning functionality.

Calendar Access

School Planner may request access to your device's calendar when you choose to use calendar synchronisation features.

Calendar access is used only to create or manage calendar events requested by you. You can change calendar permissions at any time through your device's privacy settings.

Purchases and Subscriptions

School Planner Pro purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store.

The developer does not receive or store your payment card or banking information.

Children's Information

School Planner is intended as an organisational tool for parents and guardians. Information about children entered into the app is used to provide the planning features selected by the user.

Third-Party Services

Some functionality may rely on services provided by Apple, including the App Store, StoreKit and system calendar services. These services are subject to Apple's own privacy policies.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when School Planner's features or privacy practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page.
