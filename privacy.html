School Planner: Family — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

School Planner: Family helps parents and guardians organise school schedules, lunches, PE, clubs, childcare costs, tasks, reminders and calendar events on iPhone, iPad and Mac. The developer is Mr Seif Altayar MEng, MIET.

Information stored on your device

The app stores information you enter, including child profiles and names, school names and hours, attendance days, lunches, clubs, individual schedule changes, notes, closures, tasks, childcare prices and payments, and app preferences. This information is used to provide the planning features you choose.

Your planner database is stored locally on your device. School Planner does not send that database to a developer-operated server. The app does not include advertising, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. Local app data may be included in device or computer backups managed by you or Apple; those backups are subject to their own settings.

Apple Calendar access

Calendar sync is optional. The app requests full Calendar access when you choose to connect or sync, so it can find and update events it previously created and avoid unnecessary duplicates. You choose a writable destination calendar.

Synced event titles and notes can include child names, school hours, clubs, lunch choices, PE details, reasons for schedule changes and notes you enter. School Planner updates its recognised managed events in the selected calendar and week. Unrelated events are not intentionally modified. Changing the destination calendar does not automatically remove events from the previous calendar.

Saving a plan and syncing it to Calendar are separate actions unless you enable automatic updates. You can manage syncing under More → Apple Calendar Sync, and revoke Calendar access in your device's privacy settings.

Your selected calendar provider, such as iCloud or another account provider, may sync those events to its servers or your other devices according to that account's settings and privacy policy.

Optional notifications

If you enable reminders, School Planner requests notification permission and schedules local notifications on your device. Notification content may include child names, school times, lunches, PE, clubs, collection details, notes or tasks. That content may appear on your lock screen depending on your system settings.

You can turn reminders off under More → Reminders, or control notification permission and previews in your device's settings. The app does not use a developer-operated push notification service.

Purchases and subscriptions

School Planner Pro purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. The app uses Apple's StoreKit service to obtain purchase options and verify access to Pro. The developer does not receive your payment card or banking details through the app.

Subscriptions remain associated with your Apple Account. Manage or cancel them through your Apple Account's subscription settings. Resetting School Planner does not cancel a subscription or erase your App Store purchase history.

Exported backups

More → Backup & Reset → Create Backup exports a copy of your planner information to a file in a location you choose. Backup files may contain family information and are not separately encrypted by School Planner. Keep them in a private, secure location.

If you choose a cloud storage provider, that provider may store or sync the file under its own privacy and security practices. Restoring a backup replaces the planner information currently stored by the app after your confirmation.

Retention and deletion

Local planner information remains on your device until you edit or remove it. You can remove a child under More → Family & School, or erase the app's local planner database using More → Backup & Reset → Full Reset.

Full Reset does not automatically delete exported backup files, copies in device or computer backups, support emails, or events already added to Apple Calendar. Delete those copies in the relevant storage service, backup system or Calendar app if you no longer want them. Full Reset does not cancel purchases or subscriptions.

The developer cannot remotely access or delete the planner database on your device.

Feedback and support

If you email subject-developer@proton.me, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to include. This correspondence is used to respond to your request, investigate problems and consider feedback. Please avoid sending unnecessary information about children.

Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and any applicable obligations. You may contact the same address to request deletion of your support correspondence; some records may need to be retained to meet applicable obligations. Email handling is also subject to the relevant email providers' privacy practices.

Children's information

School Planner is intended for parents and guardians. Information about children is entered and controlled by the adult using the app. The developer does not receive that information through the app's ordinary planning features. Calendar syncing, backup export and support emails can share information with the providers or recipients you choose, as described above.

Apple and other service providers

Features that use Apple services, including the App Store, StoreKit, device backups and Calendar accounts, are also subject to Apple's settings and privacy policies. Any calendar, email or file-storage provider you choose has its own policies.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or privacy practices change. The latest policy will be published at this address with an updated date.
