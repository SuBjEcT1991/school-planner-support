School Planner: Family — Support

Welcome to the official support page for School Planner: Family.

School Planner helps families organise school schedules, wraparound care, weekly plans and calendar synchronisation.

Need Help?

If you need assistance with School Planner: Family, please contact:

Email: subject-developer@proton.me

When contacting support, please include your device type, operating system version and a short description of the problem.

School Planner Pro

School Planner Pro is available through:

  • Monthly subscription
  • Annual subscription
  • Lifetime purchase

Pro provides additional features including support for larger families and extended future calendar synchronisation.

Restore Purchases

If you previously purchased School Planner Pro, open the app and navigate to Settings → Pro → Restore Purchases.

Calendar Synchronisation

If you experience problems synchronising your school plan with your calendar, please ensure School Planner has permission to access your calendar in your device's privacy settings.

Feedback

We welcome feedback and suggestions that can help improve School Planner: Family. Please contact us using the support email above.
