Plan your school week

  1. Add or edit your children under More → Family & School, including their education stage, usual hours and attendance days.
  2. Open Week → Plan this week to set up the regular routine, lunches and clubs. Adjust individual days for one-off changes.
  3. Choose Review Week, check the plan, then choose Confirm Week.
  4. After saving, choose Sync to Apple Calendar to update your calendar, or Not Now to leave the plan saved and sync later.

For more guidance, open More → Help & About → How School Planner Works.

Apple Calendar Sync

Saving a plan and syncing to Apple Calendar are separate actions unless you have enabled automatic Calendar updates. Choosing Not Now does not discard your saved plan.

  1. Open More → Apple Calendar Sync.
  2. Choose Connect Apple Calendar if needed, and allow full Calendar access. Full access lets the app find and update events it previously added.
  3. Select a writable destination calendar and check the school week. Use Current school week if needed.
  4. Choose Add Week to Calendar and wait for the success message.

The post-save prompt also lets you start syncing directly after confirming your week. If you connect for the first time there, choose the destination calendar and then tap Sync to Apple Calendar again.

Repeated syncs update recognised School Planner events in the selected calendar and week, and remove obsolete recognised entries. Other calendar events are left alone. Changing the destination calendar does not automatically delete events from the previous calendar.

Free includes Calendar Sync for the current and following school week. Pro includes further-ahead weeks. Automatic updates can be enabled or disabled from the Calendar Sync screen.

If Calendar Sync does not work

  • Check Calendar permission for School Planner under your device's Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars, or System Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars on Mac. Enable full access.
  • Check that your selected calendar still exists and allows changes. If necessary, choose another writable calendar in the app.
  • Check the selected week and that school and club finish times are after their start times.
  • School closures, non-attendance days and days marked Away do not create school or club events.
  • Read the error message, correct the issue and try again. A failed Calendar sync does not discard the plan saved in School Planner.

Reminders

Manage evening, morning and collection reminders under More → Reminders. Notifications require system permission. If reminders are not appearing, check School Planner's notification settings, Focus settings and notification previews on your device.

Reminders use local notifications. Delivery depends on your device and system settings, and the number of pending notifications is limited by the operating system.

School Planner Pro and restoring purchases

Pro is available as a monthly subscription, annual subscription or one-time lifetime purchase. Available prices and billing periods are shown by the App Store in the app. Pro supports larger families and extended future Calendar Sync.

To restore an existing purchase, open More → Appearance & Pro → Manage Pro & purchases → Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account associated with your purchase and check your connection to the App Store.

A pending purchase unlocks after Apple confirms approval or payment. Manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account's subscription settings. Resetting the app does not cancel subscriptions.

Backups and reset

Use More → Backup & Reset → Create Backup to export your planner data. Keep the file private: it may contain family information and is not separately encrypted by School Planner.

Restore Backup replaces the app's current planner data after confirmation. Create a current backup first if you want to keep a copy.

Full Reset erases local planner data after confirmation. It does not cancel App Store purchases, remove exported backup files or automatically delete events already added to Apple Calendar.

Using an earlier version?

In version 1.0, Calendar Sync and Settings may appear as separate navigation items, and purchase settings may be labelled Pro. The paths above describe version 1.1.0.
